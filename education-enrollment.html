Who this is for

Schools, colleges, coaching centres, tuition and training institutes.

Systems here follow your customer journey, in your industry's own language, instead of a generic template.

Where it leaks

  • Enquiry details live on paper, phones and chats.
  • Counsellors follow up unevenly, and nobody sees which enquiries went cold.
  • Admission season brings a rush, then the old enquiries are forgotten.
  • Management cannot see which source brought which admission.

What we build

Foundation

A clear website for courses and classes, Google Business Profile and Maps, admission enquiry forms, WhatsApp and call buttons and source tracking.

Growth

Central enquiry capture, counsellor assignment, remarks and next follow-up, campus-visit and demo-class tracking, application and admission stages, lost reasons, old-enquiry reactivation and counsellor and management dashboards.

Enterprise

Multi-campus and multi-programme setups with routing and permissions, scoped after discovery, including a review of what student and parent data is handled and who can see it.

We audit what you already have first. We keep what works, improve what is weak and replace only when it blocks the result.

What we do not do

We are not school ERP, LMS, attendance, exam or fee software. Where you already have an ERP, we integrate where possible and only add what is genuinely missing.

Questions

Is our students' data safe?

We handle only the data needed for enquiries, define who can access it, and document it before launch. We never call a system unhackable.

We already have a website. Do we need a new one?

Not necessarily. We check what you have first, keep what works and fix what does not.

Do you guarantee leads or rankings?

No. We build the system and the follow-up. Results also depend on demand, competition, reputation and how your team responds.
