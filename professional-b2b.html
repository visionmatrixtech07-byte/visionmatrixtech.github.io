Who this is for

CAs, lawyers, consultants, architects, insurance advisors, agencies and B2B firms.

Systems here follow your customer journey, in your industry's own language, instead of a generic template.

Where it leaks

  • Referrals are the only source, and you cannot see the rest of the funnel.
  • Proposals go out and nobody follows up.
  • Renewals and retainers are remembered only when the client calls.
  • You cannot tell which enquiries are worth your time.

What we build

Foundation

A professional website with services, credentials and consultation enquiry, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp and call buttons.

Growth

Opportunity tracking with owner, stage and next action, proposal and quotation stages, won and lost reasons, onboarding status, renewal reminders and a management view of the pipeline.

Enterprise

Multiple pipelines and teams, account-level tracking, permissions and integration with your existing tools where possible.

Need only part of this? We also sell each capability on its own, such as a website, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp setup, CRM setup or dashboards.

What we do not do

We automate templates and workflows you approve. You stay responsible for legal, financial and professional content. We do not replace practice-management, accounting or document systems.

Questions

Not necessarily. We check what you have first, keep what works and fix what does not.
