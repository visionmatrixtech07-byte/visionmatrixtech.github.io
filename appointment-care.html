Who this is for

Clinics, dentists, diagnostic centres, salons and spas.

Systems here follow your customer journey, in your industry's own language, instead of a generic template.

Where it leaks

  • Enquiries arrive on WhatsApp and calls and nobody tracks them.
  • Callbacks are forgotten at busy hours.
  • No-shows are never followed up.
  • Reviews come randomly and returning patients are never reminded.

What we build

Foundation

A professional mobile website, Google Business Profile and Maps, call and WhatsApp buttons, an appointment enquiry form and local search basics.

Growth

An enquiry and appointment tracker, WhatsApp acknowledgement, reminders, no-show follow-up, review requests, recall reminders and an owner dashboard. Your staff are trained to use it.

Enterprise

Multi-doctor and multi-location setups with role-based workflows and management dashboards, scoped after a discovery conversation.

We audit what you already have first. We keep what works, improve what is weak and replace only when it blocks the result.

What we do not do

We are not hospital, EMR or clinical record software, and we do not replace diagnostic reporting or medical billing. We work around your existing systems on enquiries, communication and follow-up.

Questions

Will this replace our hospital software?

No. We handle enquiries, appointments and follow-up, not clinical records.

We already have a website. Do we need a new one?

Not necessarily. We check what you have first, keep what works and fix what does not.

Do you guarantee leads or rankings?

No. We build the system and the follow-up. Results also depend on demand, competition, reputation and how your team responds.
